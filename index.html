About Romans6

Churches and small nonprofits hold sensitive information (donor records, member details, counseling notes) but rarely have a security budget. Attackers know this. Business email compromise, ransomware, and phishing hit these organizations hard, and recovery can cost money meant for ministry and mission.

Romans6 exists to close that gap. We give our time and skills freely so that organizations doing good in their communities can operate safely.

Our name comes from Romans 6, a chapter about leaving the old behind and walking in new life. We see the same hope in security work: finding what is broken, fixing it, and helping people start fresh with confidence.

Who we are

Romans6 is a volunteer-run nonprofit corporation organized in Iowa and based in Grimes. Our founder is a Palo Alto Networks Certified XDR Engineer and XDR Analyst, has served as a Palo Alto Networks subject-matter expert, and is Black Hat trained in malware analysis and memory forensics. Earlier credentials include the GIAC Web Application Penetration Tester (GWAPT) and Cisco CCNA Cyber Ops, backed by years of hands-on experience in incident response, network defense, and vulnerability research.

What we do

All services for churches and nonprofits are provided free of charge, with written authorization before any testing begins.

Security assessments

A practical review of your network, firewall, accounts, and email security, with a plain-language report of what to fix first.

Incident response

Hacked email, suspicious payments, or a locked-out account? We help you contain the problem, recover, and prevent it from happening again.

Training

Workshops for staff and volunteers on phishing, passwords, multifactor authentication, and safe handling of sensitive information.

Student mentoring

We volunteer at student cyber defense competitions, including at Iowa State University, helping students build real-world skills.

Request free help

If you serve a church, ministry, or nonprofit and want a security check-up or need help after an incident, reach out. There is no cost.

Email us with:

  1. Your organization's name and what it does
  2. Your name and role
  3. What you need: an assessment, training, or help with an incident
  4. Roughly how many staff, volunteers, and computers you have
help@romans6.com

How it works

  • A short conversation to understand your needs
  • A signed authorization and scope before any testing
  • Careful testing that respects your operations
  • A plain-language report and help fixing issues
  • Your information kept confidential

Dealing with an active incident right now? Put URGENT in the subject line. Don't include passwords or sensitive data in email.
