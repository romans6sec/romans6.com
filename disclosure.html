Vulnerability disclosure policy

Romans6 conducts security research for the public benefit. This page explains how we handle vulnerabilities we discover and how to report issues to us.

How we disclose what we find

  • We test software we have lawfully obtained, in our own lab, or systems we are authorized in writing to test.
  • We report vulnerabilities privately to the vendor or maintainer first, through their security contact or bug bounty program when available.
  • We give vendors a reasonable time to fix the issue, normally 90 days, and we are flexible when a vendor is working in good faith.
  • If a vendor cannot be reached, we may coordinate through a CVE Numbering Authority or CERT/CC.
  • We publish details only after a fix is available or the disclosure deadline has passed, and we avoid publishing information that would put users at unnecessary risk.

Work for churches and nonprofits

Findings from assessments of an organization's own systems are confidential. They belong to that organization and are never published without its written permission.

Reporting an issue to us

If you find a security problem in romans6.com or anything Romans6 has published, please email security@romans6.com with a description and steps to reproduce. We will acknowledge your report and keep you informed as we fix it.

We will not pursue legal action against good-faith research that avoids privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption, and that gives us reasonable time to respond before any disclosure.
